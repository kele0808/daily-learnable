## 1. [esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix](https://github.com/esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix)

DeepSeek-native AI coding agent for your terminal. Engineered around prefix-cache stability — leave it running.

## 4. [open-multi-agent/open-multi-agent](https://github.com/open-multi-agent/open-multi-agent)

TypeScript multi-agent framework that runs in your own environment: consequential actions wait for approval and every run leaves a verifiable record. Describe the goal, not the graph. 13 built-in providers (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek and more) plus any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, local models included.

**为什么看：** TypeScript 里「先写目标、运行时再拆 DAG」。coordinator.ts 解析任务 JSON（verify judges 不进模型输出），scheduler.ts 默认 dependency-first，consequential.ts 只看 tool.consequential 元数据决定是否拦下，prompt/参数故意不参与分类。`npm create oma-app` 有无 key 的脚本化 demo。适合看审批和调度怎么写成可回放记录。

多智能体 · TypeScript · agent-framework · agent-orchestration · agentic-ai · star 6884
